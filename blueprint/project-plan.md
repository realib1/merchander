## 1. Problem - What problem are we solving?

Social-first merchants in Ghana (importers and resellers) run real businesses out
of WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, spreadsheets, and manual notes. WhatsApp is a
good sales channel but a poor operations system. As a merchant grows they end up
hand-tracking product catalogs, customer conversations, orders, inventory,
pre-orders, supplier purchases, sea/air-freight shipments, supplier balances,
import and shipping costs, customer payments, and delivery - across disconnected
tools.

The result: a merchant can say "I sold 50 bags" but not easily answer "what did
those bags actually cost me after supplier, freight, and import costs," "which
customers already paid for the shipment landing next week," or "how much do I
still owe my supplier."

Merchander connects the supply side and the sales side into one operational
system, without Merchander itself moving money between parties (payments are
recorded, not escrowed).

## 2. Users - Who is this for?

- **Primary beachhead:** Ghanaian importers and resellers who sell via social
  channels and manage pre-orders, freight consignments, supplier balances, and
  staggered inventory arrivals.
- **Also:** growing social-commerce merchants who have outgrown spreadsheets.
- **Internal:** SHERO platform staff who operate the multi-tenant platform
  (merchant oversight, billing, support triage, incidents, staff RBAC) through
  the `/platform` console.
- **End customers** of merchants interact through each merchant's public
  storefront (`/store/[slug]`) and, in future, official social channels.

## 3. Features - What does the MVP need?

Already shipped (see `build-plan.md` for the checked list):

- Multi-tenant merchant workspaces, auth (Supabase), MFA + backup codes, RBAC
  (tenant roles + permissions)
- Product catalog with variants, availability states, categories, specifications,
  images
- Multi-store inventory, inventory levels, low-stock thresholds, adjustments
- Customers CRM with unified cross-channel identities and acquisition attribution
- Orders (Kanban lifecycle), order items, customer payment records
- Suppliers, purchase orders, supplier balances, procurement finances
- Shipments / inbound logistics with freight mode and ETAs
- Pre-order batches with a full procurement-to-fulfilment lifecycle and milestone
  broadcasts
- Expenses tracking and profitability / analytics / insights dashboards
- Public storefront per merchant: catalog, product pages, cart, wishlist,
  passwordless order tracking, custom domain / subdomain
- Payments recording plus provider scaffolding (Paystack, Hubtel, MTN MoMo,
  Telecel) and webhook routes (Paystack, Hubtel, WhatsApp, Telegram)
- Goals & Targets engine and storefront announcement banner
- Help & Support hub (merchant tickets, system status, support-access delegation)
- Platform / Superadmin console: merchants, revenue, plans & billing, domains,
  system health / incidents, communications, audit logs, staff security,
  support inbox
- Business settings surface (profile, hours, channels, checkout, fulfilment,
  notifications, privacy, export, subscription, branches, staff, permissions)

Not yet built (roadmap — Money Pillar & Business Evolution):

- Expense reclassification with COGS / Operating / Other classification,
  auto-mapped from existing categories with merchant override
- Inflow / Outflow financial view: unified money-in (sales, deposits, delivery
  fees, other income) vs money-out (COGS, operating expenses, other), showing
  Revenue → Gross Profit → Operating Profit → Cash Position, with the critical
  distinction between profitability (earned) and cash flow (received)
- Professional invoices: generation from orders or standalone, with tax
  calculations (VAT/NHIL), shareable public view with payment button, automated
  overdue reminders, customer transaction history integration
- Quotes: quote-to-invoice conversion flow, shareable public view, Intelligence
  can generate and send quote links via WhatsApp. Full flow: Quote → accepted →
  Invoice → Payment → Order → Fulfillment
- Compliance tracker: tax payments, annual returns, business permits, custom
  deadlines with recurrence, reminders, evidence/receipt uploads. Help the
  merchant not forget, not be the tax authority.
- Payroll recording: structured salary/wage tracking per staff member per period
  (gross, SSNIT, PAYE, net), "Pay" / "Pay All" buttons that mark records paid
  and auto-create expense entries classified as Operating → Salaries. Recording
  only — Merchander does not process payroll payments.
- Business calculators: breakeven, markup, import cost, profit margin —
  auto-populated from real data where available, also available as public
  standalone pages for SEO
- Dashboard reorganization into four pillars: Commerce, Money, Business,
  Intelligence
- Cross-border commerce (multi-currency, international payments) and supplier
  (China) payment infrastructure

## 4. Data - What are we storing?

PostgreSQL via Supabase. Every tenant-owned table carries `tenant_id` and is
guarded by Row Level Security. Core entities:

- `tenants`, `tenant_users`, `tenant_roles`, `tenant_settings`,
  `tenant_subscriptions`, `tenant_notifications`
- `stores`, `products`, `product_variants`, `product_categories`,
  `product_specifications`, `inventory_levels`
- `customers`, `customer_identities`, customer acquisition attribution
- `orders`, `order_items`, `payments`
- `suppliers`, `purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_items`, procurement finances
- `shipments` / inbound logistics
- `preorder_batches`, `product_preorder_batches`, `preorder_batch_notifications`
- `expenses`, `product_waitlist`
- storefront settings, colors, hero customizer, custom domains
- `user_backup_codes`
- business targets
- Platform plane: `platform_staff_users`, `platform_audit_logs`, `platform_plans`,
  `platform_announcements`, `platform_incidents`,
  `platform_support_access_grants`
- RPCs for dashboard / inventory / customer metrics; `is_platform_staff()`
  security-definer helper

Migrations live in `supabase/migrations/` (72 as of adoption). Local demo data in
`supabase/seed.sql` (runs only on `supabase db reset`).

## 5. Tech - What stack are we using?

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript 5 (strict),
  Turbopack
- **Runtime pattern:** server components at page/layout level; ~270 client
  components for interactive UI; ~60 Server Action modules in `src/app/actions/`;
  API routes only for webhooks and one auth reset endpoint; edge middleware in
  `src/proxy.ts` (Next 16 renamed `middleware` to `proxy`)
- **Data & auth:** Supabase (`@supabase/ssr`, `@supabase/supabase-js`) -
  PostgreSQL, Row Level Security, Supabase Auth with MFA
- **Validation:** Zod
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4, CSS-first config (`@theme` tokens in
  `globals.css`, no `tailwind.config.js`); hand-rolled shadcn-style primitives in
  `src/components/ui`; `next-themes` for dark/light; `lucide-react` icons;
  `motion` for animation; `sonner` for toasts
- **Charts:** `recharts`. **Spreadsheets:** `xlsx`. **Dates:** `date-fns`
- **Tooling:** Yarn 4 (`packageManager` pinned), ESLint 9 (`eslint-config-next`),
  Prettier (120 col, single quotes), Vitest 4, Husky pre-commit (lint +
  typecheck)
- **Not in this repo yet:** the Python/FastAPI + Celery/Redis intelligence
  service and the official channel adapters described in the PRD. These are
  planned to live here or alongside this repo.

## 6. Monetize - How will this make money?

Subscription SaaS. Tiered plans (`platform_plans` / `tenant_subscriptions`):
free, starter, growth, business, enterprise, priced in GHS and USD with monthly /
annual cycles and per-tier entitlements (product, order, staff-seat, AI-query
limits, custom-domain and priority-support flags). Billing is managed from the
`/platform` console; merchant subscription upgrades run through the online
payments flow.

> TODO (confirm): usage-based or hybrid pricing was floated in the roadmap
> (Phase 2 validation) but the implemented model is flat tiered subscription.

## 7. UI/UX - How should this look and feel?

- Token-driven theme system: brand primary scale, semantic surfaces
  (`background`, `surface`, `surface-elevated`, `separator`), text roles, and
  external brand colors (WhatsApp, Instagram, MTN, Telecel, etc.) defined as
  `@theme` variables in `globals.css`.
- Typography: DM Sans (body) + Plus Jakarta Sans (display), with a defined type
  scale.
- Dark mode first, light mode as an option (`next-themes`).
- Dense, operational dashboard UI (Kanban order board, data tables, metric cards,
  modals and drawers for edit flows). Merchant storefront is a separate
  customer-facing surface with per-merchant branding, hero customizer, and
  storefront color overrides.
- **Design & UI References:** [StudioGrid Pro E-commerce](https://studiogrid-pro-ecommerce.netlify.app/) (dashboard layouts and aesthetic) and [AdminCN Sales Dashboard](https://shadcn-nextjs-admincn-admin-template.vercel.app/dashboard/sales) (sales analytics, Bento KPI cards, Recharts sparklines, and report layouts).
- No inline styles; Tailwind utility classes throughout.

## 8. Deployment - Where and how will this ship?

- **Target:** Vercel (Next.js standard).
- **App type:** Next.js 16 server (SSR + Server Actions + edge middleware).
- **Build:** `yarn build`. **Start:** `yarn start` (Vercel-managed).
- **Env vars by name:** `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`,
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`,
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`, plus payment-provider keys (Paystack, Hubtel) and
  channel webhook verification tokens (WhatsApp, Telegram). See `.env.example`.
- **Database:** Supabase (hosted project already linked, ref `jzwrfeclfnzxwmodwlfs`);
  migrations applied with the Supabase CLI.
- **Security headers + CSP:** set in `next.config.ts`.
- **Health check / cron / workers:** none defined yet.
